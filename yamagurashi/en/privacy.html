Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the game app "YamaGurashi" (the "App") handles user information.

1. Save data

The App stores your save data on your device. If iCloud is available, it is also stored in Apple's iCloud under your own account so you can continue on other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. The developer never receives or views this data.

2. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To serve ads and measure their performance, AdMob may collect information about your device (such as advertising identifiers), approximate location, and records of ad views and taps.

The App does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites. In regions such as the European Economic Area, the App asks for your consent regarding ads as required by law, and you can review your choices from the Settings screen.

For details on how Google handles information, please see Google's policies.

3. Sharing with third parties

Except for the ad delivery described above, the App does not share user information with third parties.

4. Children

The App does not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
